FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Hollyvooz is purpose-built for professional voice production — including advertising voice-over, TV and film dubbing, audiobook recording, corporate narration, video game dialogue, and e-learning content. Any workflow where multiple stakeholders need to collaborate on voice recordings remotely is a perfect fit.

Hollyvooz uses a proprietary adaptive codec optimized for voice frequencies, combined with WebRTC infrastructure across 18 global edge nodes. Voice actors record locally at full quality; a lossless stream is transmitted to all participants simultaneously. The recorded file is always the local, uncompressed source — never the streamed version.

Recordings are captured at up to 96kHz / 32-bit float on Professional and Enterprise plans — broadcast and streaming platform ready. The listening stream for directors and clients is delivered at 48kHz / 16-bit with <80ms end-to-end latency. We support all standard export formats: WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, and AAC.

Yes. Hollyvooz exports multi-track stems that are compatible with Pro Tools, Logic Pro, Avid, Reaper, and any DAW that accepts WAV or AIFF. We also offer a plugin for Pro Tools and Logic Pro that allows direct import of Hollyvooz sessions. REST API and webhook integrations are available on Professional and Enterprise plans.

The Hollyvooz Marketplace connects studios directly with vetted professional voice actors across 60+ languages. Browse profiles with audio samples, book directly into your session, and manage contracts and payments — all within the platform. Talent are pre-screened for equipment quality and professional experience.

All new accounts start with a 14-day free trial of the Professional plan — no credit card required. When the trial ends, you can choose to subscribe or downgrade to the Starter plan. Your recordings and sessions are never deleted; you retain full access to your cloud archive regardless of your plan status.

Enterprise plans support multi-tenant architecture — meaning you can manage separate client workspaces with isolated data, custom branding, and individual billing under a single agency account. Volume discounts are available from 10+ seats. Contact our sales team for a tailored proposal.
